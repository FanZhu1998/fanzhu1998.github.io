Research

Market structure, and what actually predicts.

Work on market structure, machine learning and portfolio construction. The honors thesis below asked whether the topology of an industry correlation network carries a forecastable signal about the market regime that follows it — at short horizons it appears to, over longer ones the signal largely disappears.

Honors Thesis · UNC Chapel Hill Economics · May 2020

Market Regime Forecasting using Correlation Networks and Machine Learning Classifiers

Prof. Michael Aguilar & Fan Zhu  ·  Graduated with Highest Honors

Can industry correlation networks help forecast bull and bear markets? My honors thesis tested network features across 16 forecasting configurations, with the strongest reported result at a one-week horizon and generally weaker results at longer horizons.

  • 91.7% accuracy
  • One-week horizon
  • 10-fold cross-validation
  • 48 evaluated regimes

Small-sample result. The 48 evaluated regimes leave wide uncertainty around the headline accuracy, so the pattern matters more than the point estimate.

Read the full thesis — Carolina Digital Repository

01 Method Five steps from returns to classification

STEP 01

Partition the regimes

Hanna's top-down turning-point algorithm over daily S&P 500 prices, 1978–2019: 30 bull, 26 bear, 5 rallies, 7 corrections.

STEP 02

Correlate the industries

Within each regime window, the Pearson correlation matrix over daily log returns of the 49 Fama–French industry portfolios.

STEP 03

Build the tree

Correlations become Euclidean distances, dij = √(2(1−Cij)), reduced to a minimum spanning tree scored by Kruskal stress-1.

STEP 04

Extract topology

Degree distribution, in-component degree, stress and density per network — plus the standard deviation, kurtosis and range of market returns.

STEP 05

Classify & validate

Trees, KNN, SVM, ensembles and Naive Bayes across 16 window×lag combinations, each under 10-fold cross-validation.

03 Detailed limitations Convergence, regime definitions and sample size

The MST minimisation criterion did not fully converge, so the trees may not be the most efficient representation of each network; and several algorithm-partitioned regimes are only days long — artefacts of black-swan events sitting too close to the following rally to be separately forecastable. Both are named in the paper as the first things to fix. With 48 regimes in the sample, the accuracy figures carry wide error bars either way.
